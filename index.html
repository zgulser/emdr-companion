Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 18, 2026

App Name: EMDR Companion

Summary: EMDR Companion is designed with absolute privacy in mind. We do not collect, store, transmit, or share any of your personal data, therapy choices, or session history. The app functions completely offline on your device.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

We believe your mental health journey should remain entirely private. EMDR Companion:

  • Does not require user accounts or registration.
  • Does not collect personal information (e.g., name, email address, phone number).
  • Does not track or log your usage, session durations, or preferences remotely.
  • Does not use third-party analytics, tracking cookies, or advertising SDKs.

2. Local Device Storage

All app settings and preferences (such as speed, orb colors, sound choices, and theme options) are stored strictly on your local device using native storage APIs. This data never leaves your phone or tablet and can be deleted at any time by uninstalling the application or clearing the app data in your device settings.

3. In-App Purchases & Payments

EMDR Companion offers an optional voluntary tipping feature to support ongoing app maintenance. All transactions are processed natively and anonymously through the official Apple App Store or Google Play Store payment infrastructure.

We do not collect, process, or store your payment details, billing address, or store account information. Apple and Google handle all financial transactions securely according to their respective privacy policies.

4. Children's Privacy

Because EMDR Companion does not collect any personal information, the application is safe for users of all ages and complies with the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

5. Third-Party Services

EMDR Companion does not transmit any data to external servers, third-party analytics platforms, or marketing networks.

6. Medical Disclaimer

EMDR Companion is a self-guided supportive tool designed for bilateral stimulation. It is not a medical device and does not replace professional psychotherapy, diagnosis, or clinical treatment.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

If we update this Privacy Policy in the future, the updated version will be posted on this page with a revised "Last Updated" date. Because we do not collect contact information, we encourage users to review this page periodically.
